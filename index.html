MY STORY

The story behind the ambition.

At nine, a pulmonary infection changed the way I saw healthcare.

After visiting several healthcare facilities with my parents, I discovered how limited access to medical equipment and technology could affect diagnosis and patient care in Côte d’Ivoire.

I initially dreamed of becoming a cardiac surgeon. As I grew older, however, my passion for mathematics and statistics led me towards another way of contributing to healthcare: using data and technology to save lives.

Today, I am building the quantitative and technological foundations that will allow me to turn this ambition into reality.
